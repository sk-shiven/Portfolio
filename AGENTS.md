# Jules Agent Instructions

When generating or modifying frontend code, you MUST follow the design skills and frameworks configured in this repository:

1. **Taste Skill (Visual Framework):** Refer to `skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md` for aesthetic rules. Apply high design variance, sophisticated motion intensity, and appropriate visual density. 
2. **Impeccable (Vocabulary & Constraints):** Refer to `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` for our design vocabulary. Strictly obey Impeccable's negative constraints:
   - Use OKLCH color spaces and always tint neutral colors (no pure black `#000` or white `#fff`).
   - Do not use overused default fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto).
   - Avoid generic AI slop: do not nest cards within cards, and do not use bounce/elastic easing.
   - Use fluid typography and scale grids.
