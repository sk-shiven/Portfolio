# Product

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users
Fellow developers looking at my work. Their context is reviewing technical implementations, creativity, and development skills.

## Product Purpose
To showcase personal projects and technical skills in a bold, energetic format that proves frontend and creative development capabilities.

## Brand Personality
Bold, creative, energetic

## Anti-references
No generic SaaS templates (cream background, blue buttons). It should NOT look like a standard enterprise dashboard or out-of-the-box template.

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell: let the interactions and UI prove the skills.
- Embrace bold creativity: push past safe, traditional layouts.
- Keep the energy high: use dynamic motion and distinct visual styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast where needed, respecting users' preferences for reduced motion if applicable.
